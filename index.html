CEO'S MESSAGE

It is our great pleasure to welcome you all on behalf of AL FANIYA Institutional Catering.

Since our establishment, we have been committed to providing outstanding catering services while maintaining the highest standards of quality, professionalism, and customer satisfaction. In line with Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030, we proudly support the Kingdom's ambitious goal of welcoming 30 million Hajj and Umrah pilgrims by 2030.

At AL FANIYA Institutional Catering, our mission is to deliver high-quality food and catering solutions that meet the diverse needs of pilgrims, visitors, and guests. We place great importance on food quality, hygiene, safety, and operational excellence to ensure a healthy, comfortable, and satisfying dining experience. Customer satisfaction remains our top priority, motivating us to continuously improve our services through innovation, teamwork, and dedication.

We are confident that our ongoing commitment to excellence will contribute to the success of Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 and strengthen the Kingdom's position as a leading destination for pilgrims and visitors from around the world. We sincerely appreciate your trust, cooperation, and continued support, and pray that Allah grants us success and guidance in our mission to serve the Guests of the Most Merciful with sincerity, care, and excellence. Peace, mercy, and blessings of Allah be upon you.

Mr. Tahir Yousaf, Chief Executive Officer and General Supervisor of Direct Hajj and Courtesy Hajj

Company Overview

AL FANIYA Institutional Catering is dedicated to providing a comprehensive range of professional services designed to meet the needs of pilgrims from diverse backgrounds and nationalities. Our primary objective is to enhance their overall experience by ensuring comfort, convenience, and quality service throughout their journey. From their arrival to their final departure, we focus on delivering reliable support and creating a welcoming environment that reflects our commitment to excellence and hospitality.

Our services are built around efficient planning, professional coordination, and customer-focused operations. We emphasize high standards of food preparation, catering management, hygiene, and quality control to ensure that every meal meets the expectations of our guests. Our trained staff and dedicated teams work continuously to maintain smooth operations, provide timely assistance, and ensure that all catering requirements are handled with care and professionalism. We also focus on effective resource management and maintaining consistent service standards across all operational activities.

Through continuous improvement, teamwork, and a strong commitment to customer satisfaction, AL FANIYA Institutional Catering strives to make every guest's experience comfortable, pleasant, and memorable. Our dedication to quality, safety, and operational efficiency reflects our commitment to supporting the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia's hospitality sector and Vision 2030. We aim to serve every guest with sincerity, respect, and excellence, ensuring that their needs remain at the center of everything we do.
